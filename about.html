The Spring Villa story

Comfortable stays, with room for different ways to travel.

Spring Villa Guest Lodge & Campsite is a Victoria Falls stay for travellers looking for a room or a place to pitch their own tent. The lodge's name and setting reflect a simple idea: make a friendly place to pause, rest, and plan time in the area.

We have not been given a verified founding date or founder history, so we do not invent one. What we can share is the lodge's current offer: guest-room options, camping, and direct contact with Spring Villa.
